## Timeline of Events

### 1. Safe House Arrival & Introductions
- PCs had arrived separately over the previous days, **Zan first** (already established in the safe house).
- GM ran **one-at-a-time character introductions** -- players did not know each other's characters beforehand.
- Safe house social time played roughly as scripted (conversation starters used as needed).

### 2. Stonefall Exploration
- **The Rusty Compass:** the drunk trapper's rumor fired -- *"wolves carrying shiny rocks in their mouths."* Players heard it. (Foreshadowing now live; pays off at the cliffhanger.)
- **Shopping:** Zan and Balor sought new **armor -- none available** (frontier town, consistent with script's "NOT Available" list).
- **Crimson Covenant necklace:** Balor found it in a box of necklaces/trinkets, paid **2 gp**. He **recognized it immediately** (player informed privately) and **has told no one** -- not in-character, not at the table. Secret asset.

### 3. FX's Briefing & Symbol Reveals
- Played as scripted: Weave-Stabilization Initiative, Site Gamma-7, the Arquitetos symbol (circle, three sweeping curves, one broken/thinner).
- **Zan revealed recognition publicly.**
- **Kaelyn revealed recognition publicly.**
- **Aurora concealed hers** -- the Broken Crown constellation connection remains her secret.
- After the reveal, **Zan and Kaelyn had a strictly private evening conversation** at the safe house: Kaelyn shared her twenty years of Underdark imprisonment and torture (and the symbol's presence there); Zan responded with what was done to him in the Flesh Architects' facility. **No other PC knows the content.**
- **Balor spent the session probing** both of them about the mark and was pointedly kept out of the loop.

### 4. Nyra Arrives -- and Attacks Aegon (New Beat)
- Nyra's entrance played as scripted (two-curve mark, locked box).
- Later, when **Aegon presented himself as having information related to the symbol**, **Nyra attacked him** -- she wants *any and all* information touching her parents' disappearance. She tried to pin him to a wall with her quarterstaff; **Aegon rolled high and slipped free almost immediately.**
- **FX intervened**, raising a **soundproof privacy barrier**; inside it he and Aegon talked about what Aegon seeks. Nothing major transpired, but the barrier demonstration quietly reminded everyone who FX is.

### 5. Aegon & the Decision
- Aegon's vision-of-Gamma-7 entrance played as scripted.
- **All seven accepted the mission.** FX's provisions handed over (per script Part 5/13, nothing spent since).

### 6. Day 1 -- The Waystation & Amelia (Improvised NPC)
- The party **slept at the waystation** (attendant given life as **Amelia**).
- **Amelia**: lost her family to the war in the broad sense -- some sent away, some went to fight and never returned, some confirmed dead. Her **grandson Asterion** she sent to safety in **Weaveheart**.
- She noticed the **wanted posters** on the waystation wall and delivered a live-and-let-live line to the party, roughly: *"I don't want trouble. Cause no trouble, and you'll get none from me."*
- She gave **Kaelyn a letter** to deliver to Asterion in Weaveheart, should they ever pass through. *(Letter text pending -- to be drafted with an open ending that can carry a future plot hook; GM will handle visual generation.)*
- **The music scene:** Bardolemeu performed (player supplied real music and played an actual guitar). Every player roleplayed stopping to listen. GM gave each PC a private emotional beat:
  - **Balor:** the voices in the blade grew quieter.
  - **Zan:** for once he felt he had people; the pain became more palatable.
  - **Aegon:** received a personal beat as well (travel weariness easing among companions).
- Full rest; breakfast made by Amelia; departure.

### 7. Day 2 -- Rockslide, Hot Spring, Homestead
- **Travel roll 1: Blocked Path** -- a rockslide tangled with wood trunks. Resolved by climbing. **Balor fell, taking ~8 damage.**
- **Travel roll 2: Wolf Pack -> swapped to Healing Spring (hot spring).** Found on the road the same day:
  - Knee-deep = minor healing; full immersion = substantial healing (**all seven bathed**; Balor fully recovered from his fall).
  - **All seven filled a waterskin** with the water. Mechanics as granted: **applied to wounds = 2d4+2 HP; poured into an unconscious mouth = regain 1 HP (stabilize); drunk = regain 1 HP.** One dose per skin. No expiry rule defined (TBC).
- **Abandoned homestead** (Day 2 fixed beat): hasty-departure tableau as scripted.
  - **Root cellar explored by the whole party. The shiny-objects circle was found.**
  - A high Arcana check yielded: the arrangement follows a pattern of numbers adding and multiplying -- **mathematically active but nonsensical.** It unsettled them; they did not understand it. (Hoarding-instinct seed planted.)
  - **Loot rolls (d8):** a **small gemstone (~10 gp)** and a **pre-invasion journal page** describing mundane daily life.

### 8. THE CLIFFHANGER -- Siege of the Homestead
Long rest interrupted at **~2--3 AM**, partial moonlight. The wolves approached **in total silence**.

- **11 wolves** now encircle the house in a ring at **30--40 ft**: **4 large B-crystal wolves** (crystals implanted in skulls and bodies) and **7 normal wolves**.
- **Watch team:** Aurora, Zan, Kaelyn (trance, no true watches needed).
- **Perception:** only **Aurora** spotted them -- **through her bat familiar**. Everyone else failed.
- **Surprise:** the entire party is surprised **except Aurora**, who acts normally on her initiative.
