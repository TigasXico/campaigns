## Timeline of Events

### 1. The Siege  --  Round-by-Round Summary

The 11-wolf ring (4 crystal, 7 normal) encircled the ruined farmhouse. Aurora  --  the only PC not surprised  --  acted first. Wolves approached through both lanes (breach wall + open doorway). Combat was brutal:

- **Zan** took a critical hit from a crystal wolf and went down (failed 1 death save).
- **Balor** went down, brought back quickly by Bardolemeu.
- **Bardolemeu** also brought **Zan** back from the brink (2 failed death saves, a small wolf had bitten him while downed during ENDGAME feeding mode). This moment may ease the tense relationship between Zan and Bardolemeu.
- **ENDGAME OVERRIDE triggered:** as crystal wolves fell, surviving small wolves shifted from theft to feeding (hunger overwhelming hierarchy). One small bit Zan while downed before fleeing  --  but all wolves were ultimately killed.
- **No purses were stolen.** The party defended their gold.
- No food bribe or coin-scatter decoy was attempted.

### 2. Post-Combat  --  Crystal Extraction & Aegon's Communion

Two skull shards were extracted from the four dead crystal wolves:
- **Aegon** succeeded (Medicine/Religion check)
- **Bardolemeu** succeeded
- **Nyra** failed  --  the shard cracked to dust
- One body was left untried (shard remains attached)

During the extraction, **Aegon prayed to Aurelius** (rolled a **nat 20 Religion**). His god answered directly:
> *"This crystal carries a new arcane signature  --  one never seen before. It is dangerous. It must be destroyed."*

This confirmed that the crystals are not divine in origin. The gods did not create them and do not control them.

### 3. Kaelyn's Challenge & Wild Magic Surge

Kaelyn approached Aegon after his communion, sensing he was withholding information. She attempted **Charm Person** on him  --  **and failed.** The failure triggered her **first wild magic surge**:
- Result: **Teleport at will for 1 minute.**
- Kaelyn described it as **uncontrolled teleportation**  --  blinking involuntarily around the homestead until the effect subsided.

This is Kaelyn's first wild magic surge at the table. The trigger was casting a spell in close proximity to **B-crystal residue**  --  precedent set.

### 4. Balor's Blade  --  Khar'zul Speaks

When Balor returned from being downed and attacked the wolf that had felled him in a rage, **Khar'zul's voice** cut through:

Khar'zul was stirred by the battlefield, by Balor's rage and vengeance. The blade's presence during combat is now an active factor.

## Lycanthropy  --  B-Crystal Strain (GM Design)

**Vector:** The bite of a B-crystal-infected wolf (not normal wolves).
**Confirmed infected:** **Kaelyn, Zan, Balor**  --  all three were bitten by crystal wolves during the siege.

### Mechanic

1. **Incubation:** The curse remains dormant for 2 long rests. After the second rest, each infected PC makes a **Constitution saving throw** (DC 14, +1 per failed save thereafter).
   - **Success:** no visible symptoms yet. Save again after the next long rest.
   - **Failure:** early symptoms appear (restlessness, heightened senses, hunger for raw meat, faint crystal-colored veins at the bite wound).

2. **Full Moon Trigger:** The next full moon arrives in approximately **5 days** (in-world). On the night of the full moon, a **DC 16 CON save** determines control:
   - **Success by 5+:** PC retains control in hybrid form (half-wolf, can speak/think clearly).
   - **Success:** PC transforms involuntarily into a wolf form but retains some awareness (GM controls actions, PC can attempt a DC 12 CHA save each turn to direct the beast).
   - **Failure:** PC transforms into a full wolf and loses control  --  GM runs the werewolf until dawn.
   - **Failure by 5+:** PC transforms into a **crystal-corrupted** form (see below).

### B-Crystal Influence  --  "The Whisper in the Blood"

The lycanthropy here is **not pure**  --  it carries B's lingering will. Infected PCs may experience:
- A low hum in their ears when near metal or gemstones.
- Glowing crystal-colored veins during transformation (pale blue-green).
- Dreams of hoarding, of bright metal, of a heartbeat behind a door.
- The transformation itself may cause **small crystal growths** along the jaw and knuckles during full shifts (cosmetic, but recognizable to anyone who saw the crystal wolves).

This does not reveal B's existence  --  players simply feel *something wrong* in the curse.

### Cure

Standard greater restoration / remove curse will **not** work. This strain requires a dedicated side quest:
1. **Research the affliction**  --  scholars in Weaveheart, or the Arcane Conclave.
2. **Source a specific reagent**  --  e.g., powdered moonstone from a site where a full moon touched untouched snow; or a root that grows only in dead magic zones.
3. **Administer the cure under specific conditions**  --  must be applied during a new moon, or while the target is magically restrained, etc.

GM to develop specifics as the quest approaches.
