## Timeline of Events

### 1. Morning — Departure from Homestead

- Party woke at the abandoned homestead after long rest (post-wolf siege).
- Waterskins confirmed expired — glow gone entirely, "just water with a memory."
- Departed for Day 3 travel toward Gamma-7.

### 2. Standing Stones — The Memory Reflection

- Arrived at nine moss-covered stones on the ridge. Life→death→rebirth carvings. Altar with impossibly clear water basin.
- **Improvised mechanic (replaced prep version):** When a character looked into the water, they saw a memory — **of their choosing**. Each PC described a memory as if watching it play out in the water.
- **All 6 present PCs participated.**
- Each memory produced a wave of light arcane magic: starting from the water bowl, propagating through the pillar beneath it, spreading outward through each stone in sequence.
- **Boon granted:** Each PC received **one d20 reroll** (keep new result). Expires at end of **Session 4**.
- Individual memory descriptions: [To be collected from players — GM to request via message.]

### 3. Approaching Gamma-7 — Zan Breaks

- From the ridge, Gamma-7 visible in the valley below: collapsed dome, broken tower.
- **Zan recognized the ViF symbol on the exterior and the Flesh Architects architectural style** — similar to the facility where he was held.
- **Zan sprinted toward the facility**, disregarding the party. The reason: he was upset with them for almost letting him die in the wolf battle. His trust, which had just begun to form, rolled back hard.

### 4. The Jungle — Party Separated

- Between the ridge and the valley floor: **dense jungle vegetation, difficult terrain** (not open grassland as prep assumed).
- Party attempted to follow Zan. **Only Kaelyn was able to keep up with him.**
- The rest of the party became separated due to low individual survival rolls, getting lost trying to follow.
- **Aurora dismissed her bat familiar (Bat-Bat) and re-summoned it as an owl (Hoot-Hoot).** The owl searched from above, located each separated party member, and guided them toward the facility.

### 5. Zan & Kaelyn — Heart-Warming Moment

- While waiting for the rest of the party to arrive at the facility exterior, **Zan confessed to Kaelyn** the real reason for his rush: there is a person he loves — **Sophie (Subject 241)** — who might be inside the facility.
- This is the first time Zan has spoken about Sophie to anyone in the party.

### 6. Entering Gamma-7

**Main door (marble, ViF symbol, hidden mechanism):**
- **Kaelyn figured out the opening mechanism** — pressing the defaced curve on the ViF symbol.
- Most of the party entered through the main door.

**Collapsed dome:**
- **Zan entered separately through the collapsed dome** (Athletics climb).

### 7. Room 1 — Main Hall (Surface Level)

**Facts noticed:**
- Rusty large medical beds (monster-sized — too long, too narrow for humanoids)
- Broken cabinets
- GM added extra bookshelves and two desks — party checked and looted them (loot details TBD — GM to confirm)
- Large faded ViF Three Curves symbol on far wall
- False bookcase concealing spiral staircase to underground level

### 8. Balor Finds the Underground Passage

- **Balor broke the bookshelf** to reveal the spiral staircase descending underground.
- **Zan wanted to rush down immediately**, hoping Sophie would be below.
- **Balor stopped him with words.** The speech was along the lines of: *"You've gone down with us around — imagine without us."* Zan listened.
- **Heart-warming moment:** Balor, who had been probing Zan about the mark and kept at arm's length, reached through. Zan didn't run.

### 9. Nyra's Crystal Fusion (New Canon)

At some point during exploration (timing: before the owlbear fight, possibly in Room 1 or the corridor), Nyra took a **B-crystal fragment** and placed it near the **air elemental crystal** on her staff. The two crystals **fused**, forming a new crystal with stronger wind manipulation abilities.

When the crystals fused, an **arcane force wave** was released, damaging everyone in the area. This is why **Aurora was already injured** before the owlbear fight.

If Nyra focuses on training, she will unlock **wind manipulation abilities** from the fused crystal.

### 10. Underground Level — Corridor

- Spiral staircase descended ~30 ft to underground corridor.
- **Hallway layout (as described by GM):**
  - Doorframe on left wall (closer to stairs) → **Room 2** (owlbear lair)
  - Closed door on right wall (further from stairs) → unexplored
  - Stone arch → Operating Theater (Room 4, unexplored)
- **Nyra was making her way toward the closed door** on the right wall while the rest of the party explored Room 2 — then the fight broke out.

### 11. Room 2 — Owlbear Lair (Combat)

**The creature:** A **crystal-infused owlbear** — same stat block as the prep's Crystal-Infused Guardian Beast, flavored as an owlbear. Former containment vault with reinforced walls, scarred door, broken chain anchor, gnawed bones, and a hoard pile in the corner.

**Combat summary:**
- The owlbear used **Crystal Shard Burst** (15-ft radius AoE, 4d8 force, DC 14 Dex) — first time the party faced AoE damage. It was scary and immediately raised the stakes.
- Aurora went down — she was already injured from Nyra's crystal fusion force wave earlier. The accumulated damage dropped her.
- When the owlbear dropped below 30 HP, **Maddened Rampage triggered** — it was about to use Crystal Shard Burst a second time. If it had recharged and hit the already-weakened party, this could have been a TPK.
- **Bardolemeu landed the killing blow** before the second burst fired.
- 1 Potion of Healing used to stabilize/bring back Aurora.
- **The party won. Aurora was brought back.**

**Hoarde pile:** Partially investigated by Hoot-Hoot (Aurora's owl familiar) — the owl picked up a couple of shiny objects. The pile was not properly searched. The **glowing runestone** (key to Room 5) was not found — GM may hint at it during the short rest.

### 12. Session End — Short Rest

- Session ended on a short rest in Room 2 after the owlbear was defeated.
- No RP beats during the rest yet — planned for the first half of Session 4.
- No other rooms entered or searched.
- No documents found.
- Thormvuld has not yet arrived.
- Sending stone not used.
