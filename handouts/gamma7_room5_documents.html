Logged by: E. Vance

8th of Vesper, Year 500

Assigned to Gamma-7. The Chief Architect's people run the site. Everything here bears that symbol - three curves in a circle. Nobody explained it to me. You learn not to ask.

The work is... I don't know how to describe it. Some of the other researchers have been doing this for years. They don't flinch anymore. I still do.

10th of Vesper, Year 500

Met the rest of the staff today. Torren runs the day shift - strict, efficient, never smiles. Morrow does nights. Quiet type, keeps to himself. There's a guard named Tallow who everyone seems to dislike. I'll learn why soon enough, I'm sure.

14th of Vesper, Year 500

Put in my first supply request. Parchment, lamp oil, scalpels. Torren approved it without even reading. Either I'm trusted or I'm not worth scrutinizing. Not sure which is worse.

18th of Vesper, Year 500

Everyone still talks about 242. The Shadar-kai boy. Would have been a fascinating specimen, if he hadn't escaped. Agent Korvus was furious. The Catalyst Project had their best results from him and they lost him.

I pulled his file once. Teleportation reflex, modified by crystal exposure. They wanted him here for Phase II work. They say he jumped a hundred-foot drop and survived. How?

2nd of Aloria, Year 500

Tallow was late again. Third time this month. Torren gave him a final warning - next infraction and he's reassigned to a Covenant outpost. Someone scribbled on the notice. I think it was Morrow.

6th of Aloria, Year 500

Burn pit vent clogged again. Smoke backed into the corridor for hours. We filed another maintenance request. They stamped it DENIED - BUDGET. We're shipping crystal samples worth a fortune out of here and they can't fix a vent.

10th of Aloria, Year 500

New girl showed up today. Subject 241. She seems to be working with them now - goes outside on assignments I'm not cleared to read about.

She comes back different each time. Colder. More efficient.

They send her to do things the rest of us only write reports about.

14th of Aloria, Year 500

Courier came through today. Three crates to the Primary Facility. Essay results, tissue samples, reports. I wonder if the couriers know what's in the crates. I wonder if they'd care.

18th of Aloria, Year 500

Today I overheard a conversation I shouldn't have. Learned the Chief Architect's name - they call him J.B.

I've heard that name before. Someone mentioned him in connection with the Catalyst Project. The one who brought 242 in.

4th of Tyra, Year 500

Went over the expense ledger today. The Chief Researcher drinks 2 gp wine per bottle. Subject rations are 3 sp. I don't know why that bothers me more than the other things we do here.

9th of Tyra, Year 500

The damn owlbear in the vault. MON-14. It won't be quiet. If we ever leave this place, someone needs to deal with it. It hasn't eaten in days and it's getting louder.

Morrow says he heard it clawing at the inner door last night. I don't want to be here when it gets free.

14th of Tyra, Year 500

Scout came back today - goblin patrols two days west. Bigger than usual. Recommended doubling the watch. Nobody did anything about it. They filed the report and that was the end of it. What are we more afraid of - the monsters outside, or the ones we're making in here?

1st of Raven Queen, Year 500

H. found my journal again. Left it in the common room after a long shift. He put it in my drawer with a note - 'stop doing that, someone's going to read it.' He's right. But of everyone here, he's the only one who'd warn me instead of using it against me.

5th of Raven Queen, Year 500

Met the U.D.A.R.U. liaison today. They came about their specimen - UDA-07-KV. A Drow woman. I only saw her file.

The heart - it's not natural. Whatever happened to her, it left something inside her chest. Was able to analyze some crystal samples - whatever it is, it carries a weird arcane signature. Both elemental and necrotic.

They've studied her for decades - experiments, tests, painful exams - and she's still alive.

I don't know whether to be impressed or horrified.

The liaison was polite. Cold. The kind of polite that makes your skin crawl. I'm glad they don't visit often.

8th of Raven Queen, Year 500

Something is changing. More meetings behind closed doors. Less talk in the corridors. Torren has been packing - I saw crates with his stuff. Nobody's told me anything, but I can feel it. Something's ending.

12th of Raven Queen, Year 500

The Covenant's Genesis program. I don't understand it. Generations of bloodlines tracked like livestock. They don't just study subjects - they breed them.

The Chief Architect says it's none of our concern, that the Covenant operates under different rules than we do. That's supposed to comfort me. It doesn't.

Sometimes I wonder if we're any different. We give them numbers. We log them in ledgers. We ship them between facilities like cargo. The Covenant is just... more honest about what they are.

14th of Raven Queen, Year 500

Packing has accelerated. Whole sections of the facility are being cleared out - equipment, samples, files. Everything heading to the Primary Facility - somewhere E-SE. Nobody's told me where I'm going yet. Or if I'm going at all.

19th of Raven Queen, Year 500

Another elven subject came through. Covenant custody - CC-GEN-12. Young, apparently has visions. They're interested in what he sees. His personal effects were almost nothing - just a single pearl earring, shining like a star. I packed it myself.

Strange thing for a boy to carry.

Strange thing for me to remember...

1st Thalkis, Year 500

Another batch for the burn pit today. Subject tissue. Contaminated samples. Failed crystal integrations. I've been logging these for months and I've never once written down what that actually means. Maybe I don't want to know.

5th Thalkis, Year 500

They don't call 241 a subject anymore. It's agent 241 now.

I saw her once in the corridor. I could see it - the emptiness in her eyes.

She didn't look at me. She doesn't look at anyone anymore.

14th Thalkis, Year 500

They're shutting us down. Everything moved to the Primary Facility. Orders came from the Chief Architect directly - destroy remaining samples, purge non-essential records.

I was supposed to burn it all. I couldn't.

Some of what happened here should survive. If someone finds this - know that the people here were doing things I still can't explain.

This research should be remembered, announced, shared. We've made unthinkable discoveries here, and they want to erase it all. The ignorants...

- Elena Vance
