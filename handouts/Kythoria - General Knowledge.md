# Kythoria — General Knowledge

> What every common citizen of Kythoria knows about the world they live in.

---

## The World

- The world is called **Aetris**.
- Aetris has three known continents. Kythoria is the only one most people know anything about.
- Kythoria spans from frozen northern wastes to subtropical southern coasts.
- The safest, most populated region is the **temperate heartlands** near the center-southeast, where the three capital cities stand.

---

## The Three Eras of History

### Era of Foundation (before 400 years ago)
- Six great mortal kingdoms thrived: **Drakhaven** (Humans), **Elyndor** (Elves), **Kazadur** (Dwarves), **Hearthglen** (Halflings), **Everquill** (Gnomes), **Stonefist** (Half-Orcs).
- An overland trade route called **Crassian's Dust Circle** connected all six kingdoms.
- Much of this era is lost — ~70% of pre-invasion records were destroyed.

### The Abominable End (400 – 200 years ago)
- ~400 years ago, monsters began appearing. Their numbers were endless. Their origin is unknown.
- For two centuries, the mortal races were pushed back. All six kingdoms fell. ~80% of the population perrished.

### The Rise of the Mortals (200 years ago – Present)
- ~200 years ago, the survivors made their final stand and founded three capital cities: **Skywatch**, **Emberforge**, and **Weaveheart** — collectively called **the Last Stand**.
- A **magical barrier** was developed in Weaveheart, protecting all three cities. It has held for two centuries and is maintained by Weaveheart's mages.
- From this bastion, the mortal races formed the **First Alliance** and its army, the **Dawn Guard**, and began the **reconquering** of lost territories.
- The First Alliance never disbanded. It evolved into the permanent governing body of the three capitals — the **Council Accord**.

---

## The Last Stand — The Three Capitals

### Skywatch (The Lookout)
- The highest-elevated city. Serves as the primary scouting, intelligence, and observation post.
- Home to the **Skywatch War College** (trains fighters, paladins, rangers, officers) and the **Scouts' & Rangers**.
- Ruled by a King advised by the **Council of Eyes** — meritocratic scouts, rangers, and strategists.

### Emberforge (The Forge)
- An underground city built beneath a volcanic ash wasteland. Harnesses geothermal heat for industry.
- Produces weapons, armor, tools, and unique alloys. The finest equipment on the continent.
- Home to the **Emberforge Engineering Halls** (trains artificers).
- Ruled by a King advised by the **Forgemaster's Circle** — guild leaders and craft clans.

### Weaveheart (The Nexus)
- Built at a convergence of **ley lines** — highways of arcane energy crossing the world. The arcane and research heart of the Last Stand.
- The **magical barrier** protecting all three capitals was developed and is maintained here.
- Home to the **Weaveheart Academy of Arcane Arts** (trains wizards) and the **Grand Athenaeum** (largest library on the continent).
- Ruled by a King advised by the **Arcane Conclave** — wizards, scholars, and arcane researchers.

---

## Governance & Law

- The three kings form the **Council Accord** — the closest thing to a central authority. They meet frequently; any king may invoke a formal council.
- The foundation of all law is the **Survival Code** — five articles: **Life** (no murder), **Suply** (no theft of military resources), **Allegiance** (no treason), **Barrier** (no sabotage of defenses), **Truth** (no false acusations).
- Penalties: labor, exile, or execution.
- Beyond the Survival Code, each settlement governs itself. Laws vary by location.
- Society is **militarized** — every able-bodied citizen is expected to contribute to the reconquering.

---

## The Monster War — Current State

- ~3/4 of the continent has been reconquered. ~1/4 remains under monster control, mostly in the **north-west**.
- The **Western Frontier** is the active war zone. The **Frontier Road** runs from the heartlands into contested territory under heavy guard.
- The monster army was once led by **five great generals**. Three were defeated by the First Alliance. Two vanished and were never found.
- No new monsters have appeared in living memory. Those remaining are the ones already here.
- Not all monsters are invaders. **Native monsters** — dragons, owlbears, griffons, basilisks, manticores, and similar creatures — existed on Kythoria long before the invasion. They are not allied with the planar invaders, though they may compete for territory.

---

## The Recovered Territories

### South & East
- Fully reclaimed. Home to thriving towns, farms, and trade.
- Two major ports handle sea trade: a **heavily fortified southeastern port** and an **eastern port**.
- The **northeast mining cluster** (centered on the reclaimed dwarven city of **Kazadur**) produces most currency metals and gemstones. Mining towns are open to all races.

### The Northern Frontier
- The **Frostmaw** — an arctic region in the north-east — was never conquered by the monster army. It is held independently by the **Skallfang Clan**, a northern barbarian tribe of monster hunters.

---

## The Four Barbarian Tribes

- Four ancient tribal nations guard Kythoria's borders: **Skallfang Clan** (north), **Jade Wardens** (east), **Sunforged Legion** (south), **Tidebound Brotherhood** (west).
- Each developed independently. They are not unified under a single command, but share mutual respect forged by centuries of war.
- All four contributed warriors to the First Alliance.

## Races & Ancestries

### The Common Races
- **Humans** — Most populous. Created by Aloria, goddess of light and life. Adaptable, ambitious, found everywhere.
- **Elves** (High & Wood) — Long-lived (~750 years), nature-attuned. Created by Kaelthor, god of nature and tempest.
- **Dwarves** (Hill & Mountain) — Master crafters and smiths (~350 years). Created by Thalkis, god of forge and arcana.
- **Halflings** — Lucky traders and farmers (~250 years). Created by Crassian, god of wealth and fortune.
- **Gnomes** (Forest & Rock) — Scholars, inventors, law-keepers (~500 years). Created by Aurelius, god of wisdom and justice.
- **Dragonborn** — Proud hunters with draconic heritage (~80 years). Created by Tyra, goddess of hunt and nature.
- **Orcs** — A martial, clan-based people. Created by Vorn, god of war and conquest.
- **Half-Elves** — Natural offspring of Elves and Humans (~200 years). No god claims them directly; they fall between Kaelthor and Aloria.
- **Half-Orcs** — Natural offspring of Orcs and Humans (~75 years). Tainted by association with Vorn.

### The Uncommon Races
- **Genasi** (Air, Fire, Water, Earth) — Elemental-touched (~120 years). Created by Vesper, goddess of peace and twilight. Rare but accepted.
- **Drow** — Dark-skinned elves of the Underdark, created by Lolth, the Spider Queen. Sunlight-sensitive. Highly distrusted on the surface. Few walk the surface; most remain in the Underdark.
- **Shadow Elves (Shadar-kai)** — Shadow-attuned elves of the Shadowfell, created by the Raven Queen. Distrusted on the surface. Rare visitors.
- **Tieflings** — Bearers of demonic heritage (~100 years). No god claims them. Their origin is disputed — some say cursed bloodline, some say demonic taint, but nobody knows for certain. Face widespread prejudice as "godless."

### The Blamed Races
- Three gods are publicly blamed for the monster invasion: **Lolth**, **the Raven Queen**, and **Vorn**. Their claimed races bear the stigma:
  - **Drow** (Lolth) — Never joined the First Alliance. Viewed with deep suspicion.
  - **Shadow Elves** (Raven Queen) — Fled to the Shadowfell during the invasion. Distrusted.
  - **Half-Orcs** (Vorn, through Orc bloodline) — DID fight in the First Alliance and helped found the Last Stand, yet face discrimination nonetheless. Many despise Vorn for what he's done to their reputation.
- Open worship of the three banned gods is forbidden in Last Stand territories. Their churches operate in secret.

### The Public Creation Myth
The gods created the world in this order: Kaelthor shaped the world from chaos → Aurelius imposed law → Vesper set boundaries → Aloria brought light and life → Tyra instilled hunger and survival → Raven Queen completed the cycle with death → Thalkis taught craft and magic → Crassian invented trade → Vorn granted war → Lolth provided cunning.

### The Ecumenical Council
- An interfaith council representing the seven recognized churches. It mediates disputes, coordinates festivals, and maintains religious unity.
- The three banned churches have no seat. Their clergy operate in shadows.

- **200 days** per year. **10 months** (named after the gods, in creation order). **2 weeks** per month. **10-day weeks**.
- **Weekdays**: Wildtide, Lawrest, Duskfall, Life-dawn, Fangrest, Gravefall, Sparktide, Cointide, Warmorn, Shadowrest.
- The calendar has existed for ~500 years, established by the first mortal kingdoms.

## The Arcane Weave

- Magic comes from the **Arcane Weave** — a permeating field of energy that exists across the world. Its origin is unknown.
- **Ley lines** are highways of concentrated arcane energy crossing the world. Magic is stronger near their convergences (like Weaveheart).
- Spellcasters access the Weave through study (wizards), innate talent (sorcerers), divine channeling (clerics, paladins) or nature attunement (druids, rangers).

## Drakh Ix'thar — The Adventurers' Guild

- "The Great Dragon Hunt" in Draconic. Continent-wide guild. Members are called **Ix'thari**.
- Founded before the invasion, survived the Abominable End, re-established during the reconquering.
- Headquarters in **Drakhaven** (the pre-invasion Human capital at the continent's center), with chapter houses in every major settlement.
- Functions: contract broker (monster hunting, ruin delving, frontier scouting), licensing, intelligence network, support.
- Ranks are named in Draconic after legendary beasts. Highest: **Drah Ix'thari** (Dragon Hunter).
- Adventurers are highly respected — seen as guardians, cleansers, and reclaimers, not mere mercenaries.

## Economy & Trade

- **Currency**: Copper, silver, gold, and platinum pieces. Minted in **Hearthglen** (the Halfling kingdom's historic mint, now resumed).
- **Major industries**: Mining (northeast cluster), smithing (Emberforge), agriculture (fertile forests around the capitals — the soil is exceptionally rich), arcane research and enchanted goods (Weaveheart, House Thormvuld), monster hunting (Drakh Ix'thar), sea trade (southeastern and eastern ports).
- **Trade routes**: The **Eastern Sea Route** (coastal shipping) and **Crassian's Dust Circle** (ancient overland route, restored, connects all major settlements).
- **House Thormvuld**: A dwarven arcane engineering family, the premier magical item producers on the continent. They recently constructed a **floating estate called Seidga'ard** using newly discovered magical crystals.
- **Monster parts economy**: An emerging industry — venoms, hides, scales, bones, and organs used in alchemy, crafting, and arcane research.

---

## The Dark Alliance

- A recently formed political pact between the **Drow of the Underdark** and the **Shadow Elves of the Shadowfell**. Its goals and implications are not yet widely understood.

---

## Culture & Society

- **Core values**: Unity ("divided we fight, united we stand"), resilience, contribution (everyone does their part), curiosity (recovering lost knowledge), faith.
- **Death & memory**: Memory shrines — candles lit for fallen loved ones. Death is close; life is celebrated grandly.
- **Education**: Public academies (ages 6–12), then apprenticeship or military service (12–18). Higher education at Weaveheart Academy or Skywatch War College.
- **Arts**: Skywatch Chorus (festival choir), Emberforge Operas (smithing + fire + drama), Weaveheart Illuminations (magical light shows).
- **Social standing**: Earned through concrete contributions to the reconquering — monsters slain, territory reclaimed, communities protected. No rigid caste system.
