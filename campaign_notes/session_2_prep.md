### CRYSTAL WOLF — *Large beast, unaligned (B-crystal corrupted)* — CR 1 (200 XP)

> Malnourished to the bone, yet crack-jointed and strong; pale crystal growths stud skull and spine, cycling color every few seconds. It smells your coin, not your fear.

- **STR** 17 (+3) · **DEX** 15 (+2) · **CON** 15 (+2) · **INT** 6 (−2) · **WIS** 12 (+1) · **CHA 14 (+2)** *(boosted from MM 7 — the crystals amplify its presence and will)*
- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +3, **Athletics +5** · **Senses** passive Perception 13

**Traits**
- **Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attacks if an ally is within 5 ft of the target. *(Wolves never use flanking.)*
- **Keen Hearing and Smell.** Advantage on Perception using hearing/smell.
- **Coin-Sense (60 ft).** Smells precious metal and gemstone through walls, packs, and cover; pinpoints any container worth ≥10 gp. Small sums (a 20-gp belt purse among fortunes) don't register.
- **B-Crystal Will.** Immune to frightened. Ignores hunger, thirst, pain, self-preservation. **Cannot be bribed, coaxed, or commanded by food** (corrupted past base instinct).
- **Malnourished.** Cosmetic only (protects the Deadly math). Play the gauntness, the cracked pads, the silence.

**Actions**
- **Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5, reach 5 ft., 2d6+3 piercing. If the target is Large or smaller, DC 13 STR save or knocked **prone**.
- **Snatch (action option).** Contest: wolf Athletics (+5) vs target's Athletics **or** Acrobatics (target's choice). See §5.2.

**Bonus Action**

**Reaction**
- **Shard Flare (1/round).** Trigger: the wolf is hit by a melee attack, or drops below half HP for the first time. Its crystals strobe blindingly — each creature within 5 ft: DC 12 CON save or **1d4 force damage**.

**Death Throes.** When it dies, the implanted crystals crack in sequence with a high, glassy whisper — almost like words — and the body's shards crumble to iridescent grit. **Body shards are not harvestable.** No mechanical effect.

## 5. CORE MECHANICS

### 5.1 Breach — the Vault lane countdown
The cavity is close. To open it: **1d4+1 successful digs** (roll the quota once when the lane commits; track visibly).
- **Dig** = an Action + **DC 12 STR (Athletics)** check. Natural total **>18 counts as 2 digs**.
- **Pooled digging:** multiple wolves may dig in the same round; progress adds. *(Endorsed — see designer note.)*
- Every successful dig is **audible inside the basement**: scrape, spray, thud. Zan knows exactly where and how fast.
- Quota met → a **crawl-gap** opens. Entering costs the wolf its **entire move** (no bite that turn).

> **Designer note on 1d4+1 (your ask):** verdict — **good value, keep it.** At +5 vs DC 12 (70%/check) a lone digger averages ~5 rounds to a 3.5-dig quota; two wolves pooling land the breach around **round 3–4**; a lucky low quota (2) breaches as early as round 2, an unlucky 5 stalls past round 6. Min 2 means it's never trivially slow; the spread creates a genuine countdown Zan can play against (brace the hatch, ready a crossbow at the gap, wedge debris — award +2 DC to further digs if he spends an action wedging). Pooling doubles as your pressure knob: commit more muscle to the wall and the vault opens sooner.

### 5.2 Snatch & Flee
- Contest: wolf **Athletics +5** vs target's **Athletics or Acrobatics** (target's choice).
- Wolf has **advantage** if the target is prone, restrained, or unconscious.
- Wolf has **disadvantage** if the container is buckled/strapped AND the target is standing free.
- Success: one carried purse/container transfers to the wolf (**max one per wolf**). An unsecured pile on the ground can be grabbed by any wolf with no contest as part of movement.
- On success the wolf **flees NW** immediately: **laden = half speed (25 ft), no Dash**, no further snatches. Off-map in ~2 rounds. Interception is possible — that's the chase drama.

### 5.3 Theft-over-Kill doctrine (crystal wolves)
They bite to *drop obstacles*, not to kill. A downed PC means an unguarded purse — they disengage from a dropped foe unless nothing stealable remains anywhere. They do not finish the fallen. (Smalls in feeding mode, §5.6, are the exception.)

### 5.4 Food Bribe — flipping the smalls
Requirements: **`speak with animals`** active (Aurora can slot-cast it as an Action, 10-min duration — ritual casting is too slow in combat; nobody else at the table plausibly has it) **plus food physically thrown/offered** within reach of the smalls.
- Action: **Animal Handling or Persuasion, DC 16.** The bar is brutal on purpose — these are hierarchical animals overriding the pack's crystal-set dominance, and it takes a starved base instinct plus an immediate motivator (rations literally dangling) to do it.
- **Advantage** if ≥2 days of rations or fresh meat is on offer. **Advantage again during ENDGAME OVERRIDE** (starvation peaks once the leaders fall) — *your approval requested.*
- **Success:** smalls break off and eat — **1d2+1 rounds** taking no hostile action.
- **Tier 2 (your ruling): same offer, DC 20:** the hierarchy never breaks *against* its leaders — smalls will NOT attack the crystal wolves. But **1d4 of the nearest smalls break and desert**: Dash north, gone from the fight for good. Hunger beats obedience, but only into flight. Smalls never return once deserted.
- **Failure:** hierarchy holds. **Fail by 5+**: the nearest crystal leader fixes its gaze on the briber — it targets them next round.
- **Crystal wolves are immune.** Their corruption has eaten the instinct food speaks to.

### 5.5 Ripple Morale — while ≥2 leaders live
When a crystal wolf dies, each small within 30 ft: **DC 10 WIS** save or flee north at Dash speed (survivors scatter toward the hideout).

### 5.6 ENDGAME OVERRIDE — ≤1 leader left *(decision (i))*
The moment **at most one crystal wolf remains alive**, remaining smalls stop fleeing: **FEEDING MODE.** No more morale saves. They attack aggressively (Pack Tactics), **prioritizing downed PCs as prey** — killing to eat, not stealing. They fight until dead or successfully food-bribed (§5.4 still works, with advantage).
*Operational definition flagged: "most or all leaders killed" = **1 or fewer alive**. Approve?*

### 5.7 Failsafe — the body-drag *(your decision #4)*
If the crystal wolves **secure 1–2 pouches** AND a **PC is downed**, then 1d2 smalls may spend their turns **dragging the body** 10 ft/round toward the treeline.
- Release: the body is freed if the draggers take **>10 combined damage** or a PC ends either dragger's grab (contested Athletics).
- Telegraph it loudly the first time it becomes possible ("the small ones' eyes have stopped being afraid") — a soft failstate the players can see coming and fight against.

### 5.8 Coin-scatter decoy (player-facing lever)
Any character may toss ≥10 gp as an object interaction. Each small within 30 ft: **DC 10 WIS** save or spend its next turn chasing/scattering after the coins. Crystal wolves are immune — they're coming for the *real* vault. Reward players who decode the motive mid-fight.

## 10. ARRIVAL — SITE GAMMA-7 (ends the session)

**Read-aloud (dusk, valley rim):**
> Three days of frontier, and the land runs out of gentleness at the top of a ridge. Below, a valley holds the evening like a bowl — cold air pooling, birdsong absent. And at its heart, wrongness wearing the mask of ruins: a **dome, collapsed inward**, its ribs pointing at the sky like a hand caught mid-clench; beside it a **broken tower**, snapped at half height. Around them, geometric scars — foundations, embankments, tree-lines planted in rows too straight for nature — the bones of a place built by minds that measured everything, including, perhaps, you.
>
> Zan — you know these lines. The proportions, the materials, the way the buildings crouch around a central axis. **You have been inside a building that was drawn by the same hand.**
>
