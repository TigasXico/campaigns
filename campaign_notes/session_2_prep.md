## 5. CORE MECHANICS

### 5.1 Breach — vault lane countdown
Dig quota: **1d4+1** (roll when lane commits). Dig = Action + DC 12 STR (Athletics). Natural >18 = 2 digs. **Pooled** among diggers per round. Quota met → crawl-gap opens; entering costs the wolf 15 ft. of movement. Each dig is audible inside the basement.

### 5.2 Snatch & Flee
Contest: wolf Athletics (+5) vs target's Athletics/Acrobatics. Wolf advantage vs prone; disadvantage if buckled + standing. Success: 1 purse transfers (max 1/wolf). **Flees NW:** half speed, no Dash. Off-map ~2 rounds.

### 5.3 Food Bribe — smalls only (speak with animals + thrown food)
- **DC 16** → 1d4 - 1 (min. 1) rounds stand down. **DC 20** → 1d4 nearest smalls desert north (never turn on leaders).
- Advantage if ≥4 rations offered. Advantage during ENDGAME OVERRIDE. Fail by 5+: targeted by nearest leader.
- Crystal wolves immune (corruption overrides hunger).

### 5.4 Morale & Phase Changes

- **ENDGAME OVERRIDE (≤1 leader alive):** feeding mode — no morale saves. Smalls kill to eat. Focus-fire weakest PC. Drag downed bodies (release: >10 dmg to draggers or grapple broken).
- **Body-drag failsafe:** if crystal wolves have stolen ≥2 purse AND a PC is downed, 1d4 - 1 (min 1) smalls may drag the body 10 ft/each wolf/round toward treeline
### 5.5 Coin-scatter decoy
Any PC tosses ≥10 gp as object interaction. Smalls within 30 ft → DC 10 WIS or chase coins next turn. Crystal wolves immune.

No scripted waves — run the wolves as decision-makers working these rules:

Smalls hold no coin-lust: they herd, harry, and lookout under leader direction until FEEDING MODE.

**When deciding what the wolves do each round, weigh:**
- **Theft over kills.** Bite to drop an obstacle, never to finish; a downed PC is an unguarded purse, not prey (feeding mode excepted).
- **Upgrade greed.** A leader retargets whenever it senses a *higher-ranked* purse it can reach this turn; otherwise it sticks to its lane job (dig the breach / work the doorway).
- **Guard what you stole.** Anything threatening the digger, blocking the breach, or touching a laden wolf gets bitten — protecting loot outranks taking loot.
- **Self-preservation valve.** A leader below ~10 HP carrying a purse withdraws NW with it: the hoard outweighs any packmate. This is your main "manageable" lever — use it freely.
- **Snatch math.** Attempt grabs on prone/down targets (advantage); skip buckled-and-standing targets while better options exist.
- **Exit geometry.** Laden wolves leave at half speed, no Dash, due NW toward the lair; remaining leaders cover the retreat, then leave once *their* purse is secured or their HP is spent.
- **Feeding mode (≤1 leader alive).** Caution ends: focus-fire the weakest-looking PC; downed PCs are dragged/eaten (see failsafe §5.4).
- **Social levers trump everything where applicable:** food bribe (DC 16 stand-down · DC 20 desertion), coin-scatter decoy (DC 10 WIS)

## 8. OPTIONAL DETOUR — THE HIDEOUT (NW, ~1 hour)

**Tracking DCs (Survival):** laden wolves leak scent and — gift — **drop scattered coins** along the way (free breadcrumbs; recovering strays en route sells the theft viscerally).
- Same night, before dawn: **DC 12** (partial moon is fine for darkvision PCs; Nyra's ranger kit likely grants advantage — say yes).
- Next morning: **DC 15**. After 24 h or rain: **DC 20**.

**The site (~3 miles NW):** a ravine cut into a rocky rise behind a fallen granite slab, mouth screened by dense pines. A cold camp. The heart of it: a **stash mound** — coins, gemstones, and metal scrap sorted **by shine, not value**, nested magpie-style among the bones of starved deer. The pack barely hunts; the leaders' will suppresses the instinct, and everything starves around the treasure.

**Denizens:** **1 freshly implanted crystal wolf** (HP 22, crystal half-grown, movements wrong, no Shard Flare yet, whimpers in glass-chimes) · **1 injured crystal wolf** (HP 18, lame foreleg, Shard Flare intact) · **2d4 gaunt small wolves** (HP 7, too starved to hunt).

**Lair layout (dry-erase-ready):**
- **Approach:** ravine mouth behind the fallen granite slab — a single ~10-ft gap, natural chokepoint.
- **Lookout shelf:** low rocks flanking the mouth; guard smalls perch here.
- **Main hollow (center):** there's a hole on the ground, 2 ft. in diameter. The hole leads to a small underground chamber where the hoard lies. The PC puches are not in the hole yet - recoverable easily. The mound is 40 ft bellow the ground.
- **Den alcove (side):** shallow scrape where the injured crystal wolf lies.
- **Back crack:** a 2-ft crawl behind the mound — escape valve. The fresh-implant wolf fits; Medium PCs need to widen it; nobody fights effectively inside.

**If pursued the same night**, the laden fugitives arrive just before or with the party: the injured wolf rises slowly to defend the mound; the fresh-implant wolf panics (erratic fear-bites, tries to press itself into the stash); smalls scatter to the shelf. Injured wolf dies → smalls DC 10 WIS or cower and flee. Fresh wolf flees through the back crack below 15 HP rather than die. **The stolen purses are in the mound** — recovering them is looting, not a second war.

**The clue:** snagged on a root at the mound's edge — a **broken skull-shackle band**: a ceramic-and-metal hoop snapped from some wolf's skull, bolts sheared, empty socket stained with **crystal residue**. Craftsmanship deliberately unreadable — organic overgrowth or machine precision. Do not explain it.
