# CAMPAIGN ROADMAP -- Order-Based Progression
## Levels 3->20 | Three-Layer Conspiracy + Interleaved PC Arcs | No Fixed Timeline

**Last updated**: 2026-08-29
**Status**: Draft -- ready for review and enrichment

---

## PART 0: DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

This campaign is built on **four pillars**:
1. **Sandbox with limited options** -- 3-4 active quests at all times, party chooses which to pursue. The world reacts to their choices.
2. **Conspiracy layer cake** -- Each arc peels one layer of the onion. Small reveals, distributed and interleaved. No two lore beats from the same conspiracy topic in a row.
3. **PC arcs woven into the main plot** -- Every player's backstory is essential. PC arcs ARE the main plot, not side quests.
4. **Living war** -- The monster army is active. Travel is dangerous. The frontier shifts. The wave timing depends on player actions.

---

## PART 0.5: THE B-CRYSTAL INFLUENCE SYSTEM -- A GLOBAL PRESSURE TRACK

This system runs in the background of the entire campaign. The B-crystal influence grows and evolves based on party actions, world events, and the passage of game time. It is not a script -- it is a pressure that the party can accelerate, slow, or redirect.

### Monster Hoarding Escalation

| Stage | Behavior | Campaign Phase |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| **1. Small hoards** | Individual monsters or small packs guard treasure. Party finds scattered caches. | Arc 0-1 (Gamma-7 era) |
| **2. Coordinated raids** | Monster groups attack small settlements specifically to steal CMGMI. Frontier villages under pressure. | Arc 1-2 |
| **3. Settlement sieges** | Larger monster forces target towns and trade routes. The Last Stand army mobilizes. | Arc 2-3 |
| **4. Capital-scale invasion** | A massive, hoarding-driven monster army push targets one or more Last Stand capitals. Requires defense. | Arc 3-4 (Mid-campaign climax) |

### Mortal Infection Progression

| Stage | Who Is Affected | Campaign Phase |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **Stage 1: Subtle** | Frontier settlers, miners, adventurers with large hoards | Early (Arc 0-1) |
| **Stage 2: Noticeable** | Town mayors, local officials, crime bosses, wealthy merchants | Mid (Arc 2-3) |
| **Stage 3: Significant** | Captains of the guard, army generals, city council members | Mid-late (Arc 3-4) |
| **Stage 4: Acute** | Capital kings, high clergy, the Council Accord itself | Late (Arc 4-5) |

### Global Amplifiers

The influence can be amplified globally by:
- **Unearthing more B-crystals** -- each new deposit brought to the surface increases the ambient resonance in the Weave, strengthening the pull everywhere

### Key: The Last Stand Capital Defense

This is a planned **mid-campaign arc** (likely Arc 3 or 4). When the influence escalates to Stage 3-4 on the monster track, the monster army may coordinate a massive push toward one or more of the three Last Stand capitals (Skywatch, Emberforge, Weaveheart). The party must choose:
- Which capital to defend (they cannot be everywhere)
- Whether to fight the army directly, target the hoarding leadership, or find another solution
- How the B-crystal influence on both monsters and mortals shapes the battle

This arc serves as the campaign's midpoint climax. After it, the focus shifts from "surviving the war" to "solving the root cause" (the deeper conspiracy layers).

- **Level 20**: The final confrontation.

## PART 4: PC SPOTLIGHT SCHEDULE (First Pass)

### Rule
- **Minor beat**: Every 3-4 sessions, every PC gets a moment. Hard rule.
- **Medium beat**: Once per arc (every 30-50 sessions), a PC's plot advances significantly but doesn't climax.
- **Major spotlight**: One per PC per campaign phase.

### Phase-by-Phase Rotation

| Campaign Phase | Sessions | Major Spotlights | Medium Beats |
|---------------|----------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Phase 1** (Arc 0-1) | 1-40 | Zan (Gamma-7 foundations), Balor (Covenant hunt begins) | Kaelyn (crystal heart instability emerges), Aegon (visions sharpen), Aurora (constellations point to first location) |
| **Phase 2** (Arc 2-3) | 41-80 | Kaelyn (Underdark), Zan (Dun'vis/Shadowfell), Aegon (Paul's truth) | Nyra (second crystal), Bardolemeu (family contact), Balor (blade reaches Stage 2) |
| **Phase 3** (Arc 3-4) | 81-120 | Nyra (remaining crystals/parents), Aurora (Polaris), Bardolemeu (family/Covenant) | Kaelyn (post-Underdark), Zan (Sophie rescue), Balor (mother's cache) |
| **Phase 4** (Arc 4-5) | 121-160 | Balor (mother's story/avatar control), Odessa* (Valen), Sanzo* (backstory) | All B-touched (ritual buildup), Nyra (post-parents) |
| **Phase 5** (Arc 5-6) | 161-200+ | All B-touched (ritual convergence), every PC gets a resolution beat | Endgame |

*\*If Odessa and Sanzo are regularly present.*

### The 2-Session Spotlight Rule
When running a PC's major spotlight arc:
- **Sessions 1-2 of that arc**: The emotional/dramatic payoff happens here.
- **Sessions 3-5**: Consequences, fallout, mechanical rewards, follow-up.
- **Reason**: If the player misses a session in their own arc, they've already had their big moment.

## PART 5: TIMING & MONSTER WAVE SYSTEM

Party departs Stonefall on Day 0. Reaches Gamma-7 on Day 3 evening.

| In-Game Time at Gamma-7 | Monster Wave Arrives at Stonefall | Party Experience on Return |

## PART 6: THE GAMMA-7 THREE-WAY COLLISION

### Faction Arrival Order

1. **Party arrives first** (Session 3 start) -- explore upper level
2. **Thormvuld strike team arrives** (mid-Session 3 or after short rest) -- 3-5 demolition specialists, carrying alchemical fire. Sweeping facility from a different entrance, destroying records.
3. **Covenant agents arrive** (Session 4) -- 2-3 people. Here to secure the B-crystal sample and identify intruders. Encounter Thormvulds first.

### What Each Side Wants

| Side | Priority | Willing to Negotiate? |
|------|----------|----------------------|
| **Party** | Survive, get sample + records, escape | -- |
| **Thormvulds** | Destroy all evidence. Kill witnesses. | Only if party has something they want more. |
| **Covenant** | Secure sample. Identify party. Eliminate Thormvulds. | Yes -- they want information more than a fight. |

### Potential Outcomes

- Party hides, both factions fight and weaken each other
- Party fights both -- hard fight, high reward
- Party negotiates with Covenant -- learn the Covenant name early but become identified
- Party allies with Thormvulds temporarily -- unlikely, will be betrayed

## PART 8: TRAVEL & ENCOUNTER DESIGN

**1. The frontier is alive** -- Encounters are situations: patrols, refugees, burning farmhouses, crashed airships, interrogations.

**2. Travel is a resource game** -- Rations, water, exhaustion, equipment wear.

**3. The war shifts** -- Early: dangerous but sporadic. Later: organized monster patrols. Intelligent commanders who may want to talk.

**4. Travel speeds** -- Party will eventually get faster travel (Find Greater Steed at level 9, Teleportation Circle at level 13, potentially an airship).

## PART 11: GLOSSARY OF KEY TERMS

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **CMGMI** | Currency Metals, Gemstones, and Magical Items -- the three categories of matter that B-crystal-influenced creatures seek out and hoard. All three are fragments of B's physical remains. |
