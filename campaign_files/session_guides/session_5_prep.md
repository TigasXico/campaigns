## SCENE 2 — SOL ROMEO INTERROGATION

**Sol Romeo** (Wizard 4, disguised as Bard). Bardolemeu's direct cousin. Mid-level Romeo operative — trusted with field work, not trusted with the ViF truth.

### Who She Is

- Wizard passing as a bard (Romeos are famous artists — cover is natural)
- Sent by the Romeo family to accompany the Thormvuld cleanup team
- What she was told: *"The Thormvulds found a facility belonging to people we do business with. Go. Read the room. Make sure nothing traces back to us."*
- She doesn't know the facility was ViF or that the Romeos are ViF members
- She doesn't know about the Crimson Covenant, the Convocation, or post-gods plans

### What She Knows (Reveal Order)

**1. Who she is + why she's here (without prompting):**
She'll give her name and the cover story —arcane consultant, helping the Thormvulds clean up. Only when Bardolemeu reacts does she acknowledge the Romeo connection.

**2. The family philosophy (ROM-04 partial):**
*"Our family makes itself indispensable. We gather information. We connect people. Everyone owes us something. You know this, Bardolemeu — you grew up in it."*

**3. The Thormvuld-Romeo relationship:**
The families have a business relationship — Romeos provide intel and connections; Thormvulds provide arcane resources and gold. She's not privy to the details of what the partners want, only that the family serves "several powerful people and organizations whose names are not shared — privacy, confidentiality, the family's reputation."

**4. The anti-party squad (ROM-03 via implication):**
*"Keylor will not let this stand. He's already assembling specialists — people trained to counter each of you. I've seen the briefing files. A cleric who can silence your sorcerer. A hunter who can track your teleporter. Someone who knows about the orc's blade. They're coming."*

**5. The Bardolemeu truth (ROM-03 full):**
*"They never told you anything, did they? Not father. Not mother. Not anyone. You were the one thing father couldn't control. Melodia follows orders. I follow orders. You? You'd ask why. You'd sing about it in a tavern. You were a liability — not because you're weak, because you're honest."*

**Facts to communicate to Bardolemeu's player:**
- Sol is his cousin. He remembers her —younger, quieter, always watching
- She doesn't hate him. She might even envy him. He got out.
- Everything he suspected about his family being involved in something dark is true
- They kept him in the dark deliberately —not because he wasn't capable, because he wouldn't comply
- **Ask:** how does he react to hearing his family described by one of them?

**6. The Whisper Circuit (only if her life genuinely depends on it —last resort):**
*"Musicians. Performers. Traveling troupes. Every theater we own has ears in the walls. The artists don't know — they think we're patrons. They hear things. It all comes back to father."*

She gives as little detail as possible. She won't name specific operatives. She won't implicate Forte. But she'll confirm the mechanism.

### What Sol Does NOT Know
- The name "Veritas in Fractum" or anything about ViF
- That the Romeos are ViF members
- The Crimson Covenant's existence
- What the Primary Facility is
- Anything about B-crystals, Entity B, or the divine conspiracy
- The post-gods plan
- Why the Covenant wanted Zan

### Sol's Red Lines
- She will not betray her family's deepest secrets even if it kills her
- She gives up C (Whisper Circuit) only if death is the alternative, and even then, vaguely
- She does not mention ViF because she doesn't know it exists
