### The Gods' Nature
- Gods ARE fragments of A and C (subdivided from them)
- Power is vast but limited — depletes with use; worship replenishes it
- Gods can create/transform/destroy non-living things, create living things
- CANNOT alter or destroy living beings, CANNOT give powers to mortals
- "Divine magic" = mortal manipulates the Weave + god signs it and claims credit
- Ki is proof — mortals can access magic without gods
- Without enough worship, a god can disappear from reality entirely
