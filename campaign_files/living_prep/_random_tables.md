# Consolidated Random Tables

All reusable random tables for the campaign. Referenced by section files; compiled into session guides as needed.

## Frontier Travel Encounters (d8)

Roll once per day of frontier travel.

| d8 | Encounter | Type |
|----|-----------|------|
| 1 | **Cache of Supplies.** Half-buried satchel: 2 Potions of Healing, 1 antitoxin, trail mix (2 days), silver ring (10 gp). Inscribed: *"For better days."* | Positive |
| 2 | **Monster Army Patrol.** 2 hobgoblins + 3 goblins. Locked iron box with supply manifests + Goblin-written dispatch (Bardolemeu reads). Cache: 2 Potions of Healing, 1 Potion of Fire Breath, 30 gp mixed coin, army rations (2 days). Dispatch mentions pushback in "2 sunrises" — marginal note: *"7th cohort hoards everything. Taking orders from something in the hills."* | Combat |
| 3 | **Battlefield Remnants.** Pre-invasion skirmish site. Bones, rusted sword, helmet with bullet hole. Crystal-stained skeleton. Atmosphere. | Atmosphere |
| 4 | **Encroaching Wilds.** Wrong vegetation: spiral thorns, vivid flowers, geometric roots. DC 13 Nature: mutated from airborne crystal dust. Cosmetic. | Atmosphere |
| 5 | **Ambush from Above.** Manticore/young wyvern. Surprise dive, retreats at 50% HP. Lair: 3d10 gp, 1 Potion of Greater Healing, melted holy symbol. | Combat |
| 6 | **Watchtower Ruins.** Collapsed tower. Climb (DC 10) for view. Logbook: *"The lights out there... they move like searching for something."* | Atmosphere |
| 7 | **Ongoing Battle.** Thormvuld caravan (3 wagons, 4 guards) vs monster army (1 ogre + 6 goblins). Party: help, slip past (DC 14), or scavenge. | Choice |
| 8 | **Gemstone Vein.** Creek bed (Perception DC 12). DC 10 Mining: 2d4 rough gems (10 gp each). DC 12 Strength: decorative stone (15 gp). | Positive |

## Road Encounters (d12) — Legacy Table

For 3-day frontier road travel. Roll once per day. Re-roll duplicates.

| d12 | Encounter | Type |

| 3 | **Old Battlefield.** Pre-invasion skirmish: trenches, rusted weapons, bones. 2d10 sp scavengeable. Child's letter, never sent. | Atmosphere |
| 4 | **Traveling Merchant.** Lone trader, mule cart, opposite direction. Sells basic supplies. Shares rumors freely. Won't linger. | Positive |

| 7 | **Ruined Temple.** Pre-invasion. No consecration to a real god. Aurora's constellations flicker. Aegon senses nothing. | Atmosphere |
| 8 | **Hermit's Hut.** Old person offers shelter + meal + frontier news for stories. Speaks of strange wolf behavior, lights at old ruins. | Positive |
| 9 | **Spider Nest.** 4-5 giant wolf spiders. Webs across path (Perception DC 14). Clear nest: 1d10 gp + salt pouch (5 gp). | Combat |
| 10 | **Abandoned Camp.** Cold ashes, torn bedroll. Half-buried journal page: *"Found the facility. The door was open. We should not have gone inside. Something is still in there."* | Story |
| 11 | **Crystal Fragment.** Small arcane-reactive crystal shard half-buried. Hums when held. Aurora's constellations pulse. Kaelyn's heart warms. Zan's resonance stirs. | Story |
| 12 | **The Raven.** Single raven follows party for 1 hour. If spoken to: *"The door is closed. It will not open for you."* Flies west. | Atmosphere |
