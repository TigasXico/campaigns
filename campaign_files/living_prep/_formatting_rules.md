# Table-Ready Formatting Rules

Applied when compiling a session guide from `living_prep/`. Every line helps the GM run the session or is cut.

## R1. Facts to Convey — Bullet Lists Only

**NEVER** descriptive paragraphs or read-aloud text. Write `**facts to convey:**` bullet lists. The GM weaves them into narration.

| Instead of... | Write... |
|---|---|
| *"The facility crouches in the valley like a wounded animal..."* | **Arrival facts - convey from the ridge:** Valley below: collapsed dome center (stone ribs clawing sky). Broken tower snapped at half-height, leaning east. No smoke, no movement, no sound. |

## R2. GM-Facing Emotional Beats

**NEVER** second-person narration meant to be read aloud. Write `**Facts to communicate:**` + `**Ask:** what do they do?`

| Instead of... | Write... |
|---|---|
| *"Zan — you've known for sixteen years what they did..."* | **Facts to communicate (to Zan's player):** First time seeing his subject number in an official document. They had a system, a process, and he was processed. **Ask:** what does he do? |

## R3. Room Descriptions = Bullet Facts + Map Layout Checklist

1. `[Location] - facts to convey:` — bullet list
2. `Map Layout:` with `[ ]` checkboxes: room size in squares, entry points, items to draw, lighting, terrain

## R4. NPCs = Name, Race, Age, One Distinctive Feature Only

Single table row, four fields max. No "Role," "Demeanor," "Knows," or "Hook" fields.

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Gender** | Male |
| **Age** | Late 40s |
| **Distinctive Feature** | Missing left ear — clean cut, old scar. Brass earring on remaining ear. |

## R5. No Pacing Timers / Session Structure Table

No "Part 1: 30-45 min" breakdowns. Document flows by location/scene order. GM uses their own judgment.

## R6. No Quick Reference Card / Summary Section

No duplicated summary at end. Embed all reference info (DCs, loot, NPC names, room summaries) in the sections where used.

## R7. No Draft Notes / Flagged Markers

No `[Flagged for review]`, `[Draft]`, `[Discuss with GM]`. Resolve before compiling.

## R8. No Timeline Pressure Overhead (Unless Actionable at Table)

No multi-paragraph analysis of timeline windows. If a timer is relevant, single line under the relevant section:

> *Full moon in 4 days.*

## R9. Skill Checks = DC Tables Only

| DC | Check | Result |
|----|-------|--------|
| 12 | Nature/Religion | Funerary rite site. Pre-dates all known religions. Safe. |
| 15 | Perception | Beneath the altar: 3 bone tokens (skull, leaf, star). |
