- Nine moss-covered stones. Life→death→rebirth carvings. Low altar with rain-basin of impossibly clear water.

- Each memory produced a wave of light arcane magic: starting from the water bowl, propagating through the pillar beneath it, spreading outward through each stone in sequence.
