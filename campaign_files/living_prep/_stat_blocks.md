## Crystal Wolf

*Large beast, CR 1 (200 XP)*

**AC** 14 (natural) | **HP** 37 (5d10+10) | **Speed** 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 17 (+3) | 15 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

**Skills** Perception +3, Athletics +5 | **Passive Perception** 13

**Traits:**
- Pack Tactics
- Keen Hearing and Smell
- **Coin-Sense (60 ft):** Detects gold/silver/platinum coinage within 60 ft. Ranks targets by total gold value carried.
- Immune to fear and food-based bribes.
- Malnourished (cosmetic — ribs showing, gaunt).

**Actions:**
- **Bite:** +5, 5 ft, 2d6+3 piercing. DC 13 Str or prone.
- **Snatch:** Athletics +5 vs target's Athletics/Acrobatics (advantage vs prone/down, disadvantage if buckled + standing). Max 1 purse per wolf. Success → flees NW at half speed, no Dash.

**Bonus Action — Metal Lure:** Unsecured coin/metal within 10 ft drags 5 ft toward wolf. Strapped purse tears free if owner prone/restrained/unconscious.

**Reaction — Shard Flare (1/round):** When hit by melee or first time below half HP. 5-ft burst: DC 12 Con or 1d4 force.

**Death Throes:** Crystals crack with glassy whisper. Body shards crumble to iridescent grit — unharvestable. Skull shard requires DC 15 Medicine (Nature/Arcana OK with justification) to extract. Healer's kit/jeweler's tools = advantage. Fail by 5+ = shard cracks to dust. 10 min extraction.

**Skull Shard Properties:** Warm, liquid-swirl interior, color-cycles. Hums near other shards/crystals. Kaelyn's crystal heart warms. Zan's teleport marks prickle.

## Crystal-Infused Owlbear (Gamma-7 Gatekeeper)

Defeated in Session 3. Flavor: owlbear. Stats below.

*Large monstrosity (experiment), neutral evil — CR 4 (1,100 XP)*

**AC** 14 (natural; +2 vs ranged = effective 16 vs ranged) | **HP** 97 (13d10+26) | **Speed** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 20 (+5) | 12 (+1) | 17 (+3) | 4 (-3) | 13 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

**Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +5
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing from nonmagical attacks
**Condition Immunities:** Frightened, charmed
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 11

**Traits:**
- **Crystal-Infused Hide:** +2 AC against ranged attacks.
- **Hoarding Instinct:** Disadvantage on Wis saves. Cannot be persuaded/intimidated to leave its hoard.
- **Maddened Rampage:** When reduced to ≤30 HP, enters frenzy. Discharges Crystal Shard Burst immediately + one extra attack/round (no disadvantage). Frenzy lasts until end of combat.

**Multiattack:** Two attacks: one claws + one bite.

- **Claws:** +7, 5 ft, 13 (2d8+5) slashing.
- **Bite:** +7, 5 ft, 10 (1d10+5) piercing.
- **Crystal Shard Burst (Recharge 5-6):** 15-ft radius. DC 14 Dex save: 18 (4d8) force on fail, half on success. After use: crystals dim, one level exhaustion (speed -10 ft) until end of next turn.
