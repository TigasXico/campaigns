## Wolf Siege (Session 2 — As Played)

### Setup

**Time:** ~2-3 AM. Long rest interrupted. Partial moonlight. Fire is ash. Roof open to sky.

**What the party perceived:**
- Aurora only (via bat familiar): ring of 11 wolves at 30-40 ft, totally silent, perfectly spaced, encircling the house.
- 4 large — crystal growths on skull and shoulders, cycling pale colors.
- 7 normal wolves — gaunt, ribs showing, absolutely still.
- Behind the house: exposed basement wall dug open — fresh dirt, cavity in progress.

**Perception:** only Aurora spotted them. Everyone else failed.
**Surprise:** all PCs surprised except Aurora (acts normally on initiative).

Ruined fireplace along top wall — climbable to small surviving second-floor section (top-left corner). Trees surround clearing. Grid = 5-ft squares. Flanking optional rule active (+ crits = max dice + roll, both ways).

### Combat Summary

- **Zan** took critical hit from crystal wolf → went down (failed 1 death save).
- **Balor** went down → brought back by Bardolemeu.
- **Bardolemeu** brought **Zan** back from brink (2 failed death saves — small wolf bit him while downed during ENDGAME feeding mode). This may have eased Zan-Bardolemeu tension.
- **ENDGAME OVERRIDE triggered:** as crystal wolves fell, surviving small wolves shifted from theft to feeding (hunger overwhelming hierarchy). One small bit Zan while downed before fleeing.
- **No purses were stolen.** Party defended their gold.
- **All 11 wolves killed.**
- No food bribe or coin-scatter decoy attempted.

### Wolf Tactics Reference (GM)

- **Theft over kills.** Bite to drop obstacles, never to finish (feeding mode excepted).
- **Upgrade greed.** Leader retargets higher-ranked purse if reachable this turn.
- **Guard what you stole.** Anything threatening digger, blocking breach, or touching laden wolf gets bitten.
- **Self-preservation.** Leader below ~10 HP carrying purse withdraws NW.
- **Snatch math.** Advantage on prone/down targets; skip buckled-and-standing while better options exist.
- **Feeding mode (≤1 leader alive):** caution ends. Focus-fire weakest PC. Downed PCs dragged/eaten.

### Crystal Extraction

- **Aegon** succeeded (Medicine/Religion)
- **Bardolemeu** succeeded
- **Nyra** failed — shard cracked to dust

**Shard properties:** Warm, liquid-swirl interior, color-cycles. Hum near each other. Kaelyn's crystal heart warms. Zan's teleport marks prickle.

### Aegon's Communion

Aegon prayed to Aurelius during extraction (nat 20 Religion). Aurelius answered directly:
> *"This crystal carries a new arcane signature — one never seen before. It is dangerous. It must be destroyed."*

**Confirmed:** crystals are not divine in origin. Gods did not create them and do not control them.

### Kaelyn's Wild Magic Surge

Kaelyn approached Aegon after communion, sensing he withheld information. Attempted Charm Person — **failed.** Triggered **first wild magic surge:**
- Result: **Teleport at will for 1 minute.**
- Described as uncontrolled — blinking involuntarily around homestead until effect subsided.
- **Trigger:** spellcasting in proximity to B-crystal residue. Precedent set.

### Khar'zul Speaks

When Balor returned from being downed and attacked the wolf that felled him in rage, Khar'zul's voice cut through:
> *"DESTROY"* — hellish whisper.

Khar'zul stirred by battlefield, Balor's rage and vengeance. Blade's presence during combat now an active factor. **Stage 2 unlocked.**

## Lycanthropy — B-Crystal Strain

**Vector:** Bite of B-crystal-infected wolf (not normal wolves).
**Confirmed infected:** Kaelyn, Zan, Balor (bitten by crystal wolves during siege).

### Mechanic

1. **Incubation:** Curse dormant for 2 long rests. After second rest, DC 14 Con save (+1 per failed save thereafter).
   - **Success:** no visible symptoms. Save again after next long rest.
   - **Failure:** early symptoms — restlessness, heightened senses, hunger for raw meat, faint crystal-colored veins at bite wound.

2. **Full Moon Trigger** (~4 days from Session 3 start): DC 16 Con save.
   - **Success by 5+:** retain control in hybrid form (half-wolf, can speak/think clearly).
   - **Success:** involuntary wolf form, some awareness (GM controls, PC can attempt DC 12 Cha save each turn to direct).
   - **Failure:** full wolf, lose control — GM runs werewolf until dawn.
   - **Failure by 5+:** **crystal-corrupted** form.

### B-Crystal Influence ("The Whisper in the Blood")

Lycanthropy carries B's lingering will. Infected PCs may experience:
- Low hum near metal or gemstones.
- Glowing crystal-colored veins during transformation (pale blue-green).
- Dreams of hoarding, bright metal, a heartbeat behind a door.
- Small crystal growths along jaw and knuckles during full shifts (cosmetic, recognizable to anyone who saw crystal wolves).

### Cure

Standard greater restoration / remove curse will **not** work. Requires dedicated side quest:
1. Research affliction — scholars in Weaveheart, or Arcane Conclave.
2. Source specific reagent (e.g., powdered moonstone from untouched snow site, or root from dead magic zone).
3. Administer under specific conditions (new moon, or while magically restrained, etc.).

GM to develop specifics as quest approaches.
