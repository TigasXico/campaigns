# 01 — Stonefall Safe House

Party assembly, FX's briefing, symbol reveals, mission acceptance.

---

## Scene: Arrivals & Introductions

**Location:** FX's safe house, frontier town of Stonefall. Walled compound with two hired guards (Grent, Marta), well in courtyard.

**What happened (Session 1):**
- PCs arrived separately over previous days. **Zan first** — already established.
- GM ran one-at-a-time character introductions — players didn't know each other's characters beforehand.
- Awkward social time. FX locked in his office with Thormvuld documents.

---

## Scene: Stonefall Exploration (Optional)

**The Rusty Compass Tavern:**
- Drunk trapper: *"Wolves carrying shiny rocks in their mouths."* (Foreshadowing for crystal-wolf siege.)
- **Balor found a Crimson Covenant necklace** in a box of trinkets, paid 2 gp. He recognized it immediately (player informed privately). Has told no one — not in-character, not at the table. Secret asset.

**Shopping (General Store — Bram Hedderson):**
- Zan and Balor sought new armor — **none available** (frontier town).
- Standard gear available at +25% frontier markup. No magic items, no plate armor, no spell scrolls, no poison.

**Shrine of Aurelius (Old Man Rourke):**
- Rourke noticed Aegon's holy symbol: *"Rare to see your kind this far out."*

---

## Scene: FX's Briefing

**FX's demeanor:** Rattled but composed. Mask cracking. Charm thinner than usual. Asking, not demanding — this should unsettle the party.

**Facts FX conveys:**
- Decoded Thormvuld documents — found references to "Weave-Stabilization Initiative"
- Facility: **Site Gamma-7** — three days into the frontier
- Symbol: circle with three sweeping curves, one thinner/broken
- Thormvulds were copying someone else's work — not original research
- People asking questions about the crystals, about who took them
- The mark keeps coming up
- **Mission:** Find Gamma-7. Bring back records, equipment, anything telling FX what that symbol means and who was there.

**What FX does NOT say:**
- He's scared — not of Thormvulds, of what the symbol might mean
- He's considering cutting ties and disappearing
- The symbol has been nagging at him in ways he can't explain

### Symbol Recognition Beat

Three PCs had private tells. After FX reveals the symbol, pause. Let the silence hang.

**What happened:**
- **Zan revealed recognition publicly.**
- **Kaelyn revealed recognition publicly.**
- **Aurora concealed hers** — Broken Crown constellation connection remains her secret.

**After the briefing:** Zan and Kaelyn had a strictly private evening conversation. Kaelyn shared 20 years of Underdark imprisonment and torture (and the symbol's presence there). Zan responded with what was done to him in the Flesh Architects' facility. **No other PC knows the content.**

**Balor spent the session probing** both about the mark — pointedly kept out of the loop.

---

## Scene: Nyra Arrives

Mid-briefing. Knock at the gate. Guard: *"Boss, someone's here. She's got a mark — like the one on your papers."*

**Nyra's entrance:**
- Young, practical frontier gear, pale blue-grey skin
- Quarterstaff topped with pale blue crystal
- Produces locked box, shows **two-curve mark** on underside
- *"An antiquities dealer told me you've been asking about marks like this one."*
- Box belonged to parents — disappeared when she was 18. This mark is her only clue.
- Two-curve vs FX's three-curve: clearly related, same origin, different piece.

**Why Nyra stays:** Box connected to FX's mystery. FX offers resources/protection. Needs help finding three more elemental crystals — party of seven better than alone. Gamma-7 is on the way.

### Nyra Attacks Aegon (New Beat — As Played)

Later, when Aegon presented himself as having symbol-related information, **Nyra attacked him**. She wants any and all information touching her parents' disappearance. Tried to pin him to a wall with quarterstaff. **Aegon rolled high, slipped free immediately.**

**FX intervened** — raised a soundproof privacy barrier. Inside it, he and Aegon talked. Nothing major transpired, but the barrier reminded everyone who FX is.

---

## Scene: Aegon Arrives

Shortly after Nyra settles. Guard sounds more tense: *"Boss... there's a paladin outside. Just standing there. Won't say what he wants."*

**Aegon's entrance:**
- Armed, armored, suspicious. Studies the room before speaking.
- *"I'm Aegon. Paladin of the Order of the Scales. I've been dreaming of a place — ruin with a broken tower and a collapsed dome. A merchant told me it's called Site Gamma-7. I came to find someone who knows the way."*
- FX goes quiet. Pulls out folder with symbol. Aegon studies it: *"I haven't seen this mark before. But the place in my dreams... that's your facility."*

**Why Aegon stays:** Vision led him here. Mystery and vision connected. Order would not approve — he follows his own path.

---

## Scene: The Decision

FX leans back: *"I need people I can trust for this. Are you in?"*

**All seven accepted.**

## Private Tells (GM Reference)

### Zan
Facts to communicate: during his time in the Flesh Architects' facility, a door at the end of a corridor — heavy, reinforced, with this exact mark. Never saw behind it. Never asked. Remembers the cold feeling. Seeing it now dredges up a buried question: what was in that room? Player chooses to reveal or keep.

### Kaelyn
Facts to communicate: this mark was carved into her prison cell wall in the Underdark. She stared at it for 20 years. Drow researchers had it embroidered on sleeves. She assumed House crest or Lolthite symbol. Seeing it here — on human mob boss's paper in a frontier safe house — she realizes she never actually knew what it meant. Player chooses to reveal or keep.

### Aurora
Facts to communicate: one of her hidden constellations — "the Broken Crown" — resembles this mark. Not exactly (constellation has three complete curves; this symbol has one thinner, almost broken). But pattern unmistakably similar. Never seen this symbol in the physical world before. The stars were showing her something real. Player chooses to reveal or keep.

### Bardolemeu
No private tell was given. The lute compartment idea from prep was not used — may or may not become canon later.
