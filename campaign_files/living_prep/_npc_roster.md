### Chief Architect
- Named Flesh Architects leadership figure.

- Junior researcher at Gamma-7.
- Wrote private journal hidden in Room 6 burn pit furnace.
- Ordered to destroy samples; couldn't do it.
- Afraid of what the Covenant is building.

### Arch-Liaison, U.D.A.R.U.
- Signed the ViF sealed letter found in Gamma-7 Room 5.
- Discussed specimen transfer protocols between U.D.A.R.U. and Flesh Architects.
- Confirms Kaelyn was ViF coalition-wide specimen.
