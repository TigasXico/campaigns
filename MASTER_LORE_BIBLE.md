# PART 1: COSMOLOGY & THE ORIGINALS

## 1.1 The Three Originals

At the beginning, three primordial entities existed — referred to only as **A**, **B**, and **C**. Their true names were never known to mortals. Power was perfectly balanced between them. [GM-SECRET]

They created everything: outer planes, inner planes, the Material Plane, all races and creatures. Accidental byproduct: **free will**.

### The Schism
- **A and C**: Believed creation was flawed. Free will was unacceptable. They wanted genocide of all self-aware races and a clean slate.
- **B**: Saw value in mortal life. Refused to consent to annihilation. Outnumbered 2-to-1.

## 1.2 B's Sacrifice — The Great Sacrifice [GM-SECRET]

Unable to stop A and C directly, **B sacrificed itself** — shattering its form and scattering its body, essence, and power across all creation. This gave mortals the means to fight back.

### What Was Created:
1. **The Arcane Weave**: Permeating field of magical energy across all planes. Source of all magic.
2. **Ley Lines**: Concentrated highways of arcane energy that formed over time.
3. **Currency Metals**: Gold, silver, copper, platinum — physical remains of B. Each piece is a fragment of a primordial being. This is why costly spell components are consumed.
4. **Gemstones**: Also fragments of B's physical remains.
5. **Arcane Energy in Nature**: Trees, stone, water, living creatures absorbed residual energy — source of primal/druidic magic. All mortals have **latent arcane potential**.
6. **Arcane-Reactive Crystals**: Over millennia, trace amounts of B's essence condensed into physical crystals (see Part 4).

### The Protective Shield [GM-SECRET]
B's sacrifice implemented a 100-year protective effect on all planes. Because the gods are fragments of A and C (which carry Original arcane power), neither the Originals nor the gods could interfere directly with the planes while the shield held. After 100 years, the protection diminished — the gods can now interact, though only to a limited degree.

### Divine Magic Exception [GM-SECRET]
Divine magic bypasses the shield because its true source is the **mortal caster themselves**. The god merely weaves their arcane signature into the magic the mortal manifests, then lies that the powers are god-given.

## 1.3 A and C Subdivide into Gods

After B's sacrifice, A and C subdivided themselves into **10 lesser celestial beings (the gods)**. Strategic choice: by becoming many, they could better monitor, manipulate, and control the mortal races who now possessed magic.

Thus began the long game: control through religion, harvest of faith (which strengthens them), division among mortals, and acting from shadows.

## 1.4 The 100 Years (Year -5 to Year 100)

During this period the shield was active:
- Mortal civilizations developed without divine interference
- The six Mortal Kingdoms were founded (Year 3)
- Churches were established (Year 18) — gods began harvesting faith
- The Drakh Ix'thar (Adventurers' Guild) was founded (Year 74-134)

[GM-SECRET] The gods could only observe, not act directly. They used this time to plan the monster invasion and the fabricated creation myth.

## 1.5 Cosmological Structure

- **Inner Planes** (Elemental): First experiments. Raw Fire, Water, Earth, Air. Used to build the Material Plane.
- **Outer Planes**: Experiments in consciousness, free will, morality, and ethics.
- **Three Anchors**: Ethereal Plane, Feywild, Shadowfell — created to stabilize the Material Plane. A triad echoing the three Originals.
  - Feywild: Life, Emotion, Chaos
  - Shadowfell: Death, Stillness, Shadow
  - Ethereal: Permeability, Emotionlessness
- **Astral Plane**: Connective fabric tying all planes together. Enables planar travel, soul passage.
- **Material Plane (Aetris)**: The final success — stable, self-sustaining ecosystems, evolving mortal races, true self-awareness. Kythoria is a continent on Aetris.

[GM-SECRET] The Outer Planes are deliberately crafted experiments by A, B, and C, searching for the perfect recipe for mortal consciousness.

# PART 3: THE CRIMSON COVENANT & FLESH ARCHITECTS

## 3.1 The Crimson Covenant [GM-SECRET unless noted]

### Origins
Founded by dissident scholars/priests/arcanists of the First Alliance who witnessed Khar'zul's sealing. They realized the ritual did not destroy Khar'zul — it **preserved** him. The prison was a chrysalis; Khar'zul was evolving into something greater. They disappeared into shadows with sealing fragments and forbidden knowledge.

### Ultimate Goal
Destroy the gods by resurrecting **B** in weakened, controlled form. Use B to destroy A and C's fragments.

### Philosophy
Do not worship Khar'zul. They study him. Believe they can harness his power. Two possible futures: Chaos (uncontrolled return) or Ascension (controlled rebirth in a mortal vessel).

### Structure
- **Inner Circle (7)**: True leaders. Only ones who know Khar'zul's full history and Project Genesis. Identities: most guarded secret.
- **Apostles (12)**: Execute Inner Circle's will. Extremely powerful, semi-independent. Permanent missions: recover artifacts, eliminate witnesses, hunt Balor.
- **Cardinals**: Regional control. Recruit, finance, safehouses. Do not know Inner Circle exists.
- **Inquisitors**: Elite assassins, hunters, detectives.
- **Seekers**: Ruin specialists, ancient tongues, magic seals, demons.
- **Faithful**: Common cultists, guards, mercenaries. Know only fragments.

### Research Divisions
- **The Flesh Architects** (see 3.2)
- Project Genesis (see below)

### Blackmail Leverage [GM-SECRET]
The Covenant knows the gods orchestrated the invasion. They hold a **Contingency Artifact** — a magical device reset daily by all higher-ranking members' combined signatures. If reset fails, it broadcasts the truth to every mortal: about the Originals, the invasion conspiracy, B, the resurrection ritual. The gods have searched centuries without finding it. Cold war: neither side can push too hard.

### Current Activity
- Expanding crystal research and resurrection ritual refinement
- Recruiting members, embedding in monster army
- Acquiring weapons and artifacts
- Evading gods' hunters
- **Hunting Balor Skallfang and Khar'zul's blade**

### Monster Army Connection
Intelligent monster leadership has recruited Covenant members. Embedded operatives research crystals using monster-provided test subjects. Symbiotic but tense relationship.

### Project Genesis [GM-SECRET]
Centuries-spanning objective: create a mortal vessel capable of containing Khar'zul's essence. Using fragments of living flesh from Khar'zul's sealed blade:
- **Humans**: Perished within days
- **Elves**: Lost their minds
- **Dwarves**: Rejected corruption outright

**Unintended byproducts:**
- **Drow Elves**: Elves grafted with demonic tissue, survived with sunlight sensitivity, fled to Underdark
- **Shadow Elves (Shadar-kai)**: Transformation attuned them to Shadowfell
- **Tieflings**: Human subjects showing greatest resistance

**The Perfect Vessel**: Found in the bloodline of **Balor's mother** — a descendant of early Project Genesis survivors. Her blood carried the exact genetic markers. From her, **Balor Skallfang** was born.

**The Theft**: Balor's mother killed three guards, retrieved Khar'zul's blade from the Covenant's vault, and fled into the northern wilderness with her newborn son. She left Balor with the Skallfang Clan, drawing the Covenant's hunters away.

**The Cover Story**: The Covenant fabricated the "Apostle" backstory for Balor's mother — in truth, she was a prisoner who escaped.

### The Covenant & Paul's Murder [GM-SECRET]
The Order of the Scales framed the Crimson Covenant for Paul's murder. The Covenant would never have killed Paul — as a B-touched visionary, he would have been a valuable asset.

## 3.2 The Flesh Architects (Arquitetos da Carne)

### Relationship
Semi-autonomous research division within the Crimson Covenant. Report to Inner Circle through Cardinals. Funded by Covenant, subjects sourced from Covenant operations.

### The Catalyst Project
Focused on understanding and weaponizing Shadar-kai teleportation using **B Crystals**.

**Methods**:
1. **The Void Drain**: Subjects submerged in icy alchemical fluids simulating Shadowfell. Kept until lungs gave out, revived at last second — measuring dark energy released near death.
2. **Arcane Dissection**: Incisions along spine without anesthesia. Silver wires and poisoned needles inserted into nervous system trying to extract teleportation reflex.
3. **The Catalyst Procedure (Breakthrough)**: Heated B Crystals placed directly on naked skin. Crystals burned and merged with the subject's aura. The agony forced survival instinct to absorb the magic, permanently modifying teleportation.

**Subjects**:
- **Subject 241 — Sophie (unknown race)**: Fellow prisoner. Bonded with Subject 242. Fate unknown.
- **Subject 242 — Zan**: Shadar-kai. Teleportation permanently modified by B Crystals. Escaped at age 13. Now B-touched.

[GM-SECRET] The Flesh Architects maintain detailed records of every subject and experiment. If recovered, these records would reveal the Covenant's full history of atrocities.

### Current Status
The Flesh Architects continue operating in shadows. Research has expanded to:
- B Crystal integration in non-Shadar-kai subjects
- Teleportation replication
- Weaponized teleportation inhibitors/jammers
- Long-range tracking of B-touched individuals

A network of hidden facilities exists. Zan seeks to destroy them and rescue Sophie.

# PART 4: THE ARCANE WEAVE & B-CRYSTALS

## 4.1 The Arcane Weave [PUBLIC framework, GM-SECRET origin]

The weave is the permeating field of magical energy across all planes. It is the **residual essence of B** after its sacrifice.

[GM-SECRET] The weave carries a **subconscious echo** of B -- a lingering, instinctual desire to become whole again. This is not a conscious mind; it is a pull, an impulse buried in magic itself.

### How Magic Works
- **Arcane casters** (wizards, sorcerers, bards): Draw directly from the weave. Wizards through study and precision; sorcerers innately through bloodline or accident.
- **Divine casters** (clerics, paladins): The mortal manifests magic; the god merely weaves their signature into it, then claims the power is god-given. [GM-SECRET]
- **Primal casters** (druids, rangers): Draw from nature's absorbed arcane energy.
- **Monks**: Ki is the raw, unfiltered essence of B flowing through them, accessed through discipline. [GM-SECRET]

### Spell Components [GM-SECRET]
Material components with monetary cost are **fragments of B's physical remains** (gold, silver, gemstones). The spell channels energy through them as focal points. This is why costly components are consumed.

### B's Lingering Will -- The Hoarding Instinct [GM-SECRET]
The weave's influence manifests as:
- Creatures (especially monsters) near concentrated arcane energy develop compulsive **hoarding behavior**
- They gather CMGMI (Currency Metals, Gemstones, and Magical Items)
- Larger hoards create stronger pull -- self-feeding cycle
- **Dragons** are especially susceptible due to deep arcane connection
- The effect is beginning to affect **mortals** at campaign start

### Ley Lines
Arcane energy flows through paths of least resistance. Ley lines are concentrated highways. Magic is easier near convergences. Areas far from them feel magically impoverished.

## 4.2 Arcane-Reactive Crystals (B-Crystals) [PUBLIC]

### Physical Description
- Transparent with liquid-like interior that constantly shifts and swirls
- Flash and shift between different colors every few seconds
- Completely solid when touched/cut despite liquid appearance
- Size range: tiny grains to massive formations affecting entire regions

### What Are B-Crystals?
B-crystals are **condensed physical remnants of Entity B's essence**. When a living creature comes into proximity with a B-crystal, the crystal **automatically connects** to the natural arcane energy that flows through all living things. This connection is the mechanism of "infection" -- the crystal's influence enters the creature through this arcane link.

The crystals are **not alive** and carry no conscious intent. The influence they radiate is a subconscious echo of B's desire to become whole again -- a residual impulse, not a planning mind.

### Key Terminology: CMGMI
CMGMI (Currency Metals, Gemstones, and Magical Items) are the three categories of matter that B-crystal-influenced creatures seek out and hoard. This is because all three are **fragments of B's physical remains** scattered across the Material Plane during the Great Sacrifice:

- **Currency Metals** -- Gold, silver, copper, platinum
- **Gemstones** -- Diamonds, rubies, emeralds, and all precious stones
- **Magical Items** -- Any enchanted or magically active object

CMGMI are the objects of the hoarding instinct because they share B's original essence. The instinct is not random greed -- it is a subconscious pull to **reunite scattered fragments of B's body**.

### The Infection Mechanism [GM-SECRET]
**Step 1: Exposure** -- A creature comes within range of an active B-crystal. The crystal's arcane field automatically connects to the creature's innate magical energy.

**Step 2: Initial Influence** -- Very weak, barely perceptible as a faint preference toward CMGMI.

**Step 3: The Self-Feeding Cycle** -- B-crystal exposure -> influence activated -> creature hoards CMGMI -> proximity to larger hoard = stronger influence -> more hoarding -> cycle continues.

**Step 4: Escalation** -- From "preference" to "mild compulsion" to "obsession" to "total subordination."

**Step 5: Recovery via Isolation** -- If removed from B-crystals AND CMGMI, influence gradually fades. After complete recovery, the creature returns to baseline. Reinfection possible at same threshold. No permanent scarring.

### Detection [GM-SECRET]
**Infected cannot tell** they are infected. The compulsion feels like their own thoughts.

External detection is possible:
- **Detect Evil and Good or similar spells**: Reveals the influence as a faint, non-aligned resonance.
- **Arcana check (DC 18+)**: A trained caster can identify it as "an external arcane influence -- ancient, non-hostile in intent but unrelenting."
- **Behavioral observation**: Prolonged observation may reveal patterns the creature themselves doesn't notice.

### Susceptibility Factors [GM-SECRET]
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Wisdom score** | Higher Wisdom = more resistance. Lower Wisdom = more vulnerable. |
| **B-touched status** | B-touched individuals (Zan, Kaelyn, Aurora, Aegon) are **more susceptible**. |
| **Prior B-crystal exposure** | Previous exposure = lower resistance threshold. |
| **Time spent near CMGMI** | Extended proximity to large hoards builds tolerance but deepens active infections. |
| **Proximity to active crystals** | Being near an uncontained B-crystal accelerates infection dramatically. |

### Mortal Influence: The Growing Problem [GM-SECRET]
At the campaign's start, the influence primarily affects monsters. Mortals are beginning to be affected, and the effect grows as more B-crystals are unearthed.

**Progression Scale:**
- **Stage 1: Subtle** -- Frontier settlers, miners, adventurers with large hoards (early campaign)
- **Stage 2: Noticeable** -- Town mayors, local officials, crime bosses, wealthy merchants (mid campaign)
- **Stage 3: Significant** -- Captains of the guard, army generals, city council members (mid-late campaign)
- **Stage 4: Acute** -- Capital kings, high clergy, the Council Accord itself (late campaign)

**Global Amplifiers:** The influence can be amplified globally by unearthing more B-crystals -- each new deposit brought to the surface increases ambient resonance in the Weave, strengthening the pull everywhere.

### The Hoarding Pressure on the War [GM-SECRET]
The influence reshapes the monster army:
1. **Small hoards**: Individual monsters or small packs guarding treasure (early campaign)
2. **Coordinated raids**: Monster groups attack settlements to steal CMGMI (mid campaign)
3. **Settlement sieges**: Coordinated forces target larger towns (mid-late campaign)
4. **Capital-scale invasion**: A major push targets one or more Last Stand capitals (late campaign -- planned mid-campaign climax arc)

### Magical Reactivity
Each crystal reacts differently depending on school of magic exposed:
- **Abjuration**: May generate anti-magic fields, negating/absorbing magic
- **Evocation**: May violently discharge stored energy
- Other schools: Each produces a unique interaction (not yet catalogued)

### Zones and Effects
- **Dead Magic Zones**: When crystals grow large or amass in great quantities, drained of stored arcane energy, they reabsorb ambient magic -- creating areas where spells don't function.
- **Wild Magic Zones**: Under physical stress (extreme pressure, temperature, trauma), reactions become unstable -- spell effects randomized or amplified.

### Current State
The crystals are unnamed and poorly understood. The Thormvulds have been quietly studying them for ~7 years. The Crimson Covenant has had samples for ~200 years. The wider world is only now hearing rumors of "strange crystals" tied to the Thormvuld floating island.

### Dragons
**Dragons** feel the B-crystal pull more powerfully than nearly any other creature. ## 5.2 Khar'zul (The Flesh That Remembers) [PUBLIC/GM-SECRET mix]

One of the Five Generals. A **balor** from the Abyss. His physical form was destroyed in Year 296; his immortal essence was sealed within his own sword — the only vessel capable of containing him because it had absorbed his blood, victories, hatred, and corruption over countless wars.

[GM-SECRET] The sealing ritual did not destroy Khar'zul — it **preserved** him. The prison was a chrysalis. Khar'zul has been evolving within the blade.

### The Blade's Transformation
Over centuries, steel gave way to living flesh. Veins spread beneath the metal. Muscles wrapped around the guard. A single enormous yellow demonic eye opened within the blade. Now none can tell where the demon ends and the sword begins.

### The Blade's Evolution Stages
1. **Stage 1 — Awakening** (current): +1 weapon. Whispers. Eye tracks movement. Khar'zul observes and tests.
2. **Stage 2 — Smoldering Heart**: +2 weapon. Fire damage. Darkvision. Blood-addiction drawback.
3. **Stage 3 — Burning Will**: +2 weapon. AOE fire. Khar'zul treats Balor like protege. The Debt mechanic.
4. **Stage 4 — Flesh and Steel**: +3 weapon. Fusion. Permanent physical changes. Warlock level forced.

**Avatar of the Living Prison**: A state, not a stage. Khar'zul fully takes control of Balor's body. Triggered once during the Skallfang village destruction.

### The Rite of Ash
Prayer in Old Abyssal spoken over the blade while meditating. Quiets Khar'zul's whispers. Duration increases as the bond deepens (5 min → 10 min → 30 min → 1 hour).
